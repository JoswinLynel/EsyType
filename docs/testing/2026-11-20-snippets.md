## Differences from the plan

| Plan | Built | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Order: Dictionary, then Snippets | **Snippets first**, then Dictionary, then Clean | Snippet text must be protected from Dictionary corrections as well as Clean, and matching what was heard is simpler and predictable. A trigger the engine mishears is fixed by adding the misheard form as another snippet, not by a correction |
| "A trigger that clashes with a dictionary entry or another snippet is pointed out" | Duplicate triggers are refused; clashes with dictionary entries are not checked | Snippets now match before the Dictionary, so a dictionary entry cannot change a trigger |
