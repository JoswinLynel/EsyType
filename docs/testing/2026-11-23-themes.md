## 1. What was built

### Three theme options
In Settings > **Appearance**, **Theme**:
- **Follow Windows:** the default, also for every existing settings file. It switches with Windows' light or dark app mode, also while EsyType is open.
- **Dark:** today's look.
- **Light:** warm white, ink text, the same violet accent.

**How it works:**
- **Saved** as `theme` (`system`, `dark`, `light`) in the settings file. The file is not rewritten just by starting; any other value counts as Follow Windows.
- **One switch:** EsyType sets the main window's theme (`apply_theme` in `main.rs`). The title bar and the page's colour scheme follow, because WebView2 takes its colour scheme from the window.
  - The fixed `"theme": "Dark"` was removed from `tauri.conf.json`.
- **`styles.css`:**
  - light values on `:root`, dark values under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`
  - every colour written directly into a rule is now a token, apart from the white text on red buttons and the pill's own dark design
  - design system section 3.9 lists the tokens
- **The recording pill stays dark** over any app.
  - It shares the main window's colour scheme (one WebView2 profile), so its few colours are pinned on `.pill-body`.
  - The first attempt, giving the pill window its own Dark theme, had no effect, and the theme suite caught it.

### Frosted glass
Owner's addition, plan section 4.4:
- **Conditions:** only while Windows' Transparency effects are on. The `transparency_effects` command reads `HKCU\...\Themes\Personalize\EnableTransparency`. The page checks it on start and whenever the window comes to the front.
- **Off or in high contrast:** everything is solid.
- **6.1 glass:**
  - dialogs and notifications are frosted (24 px blur)
  - the dialog's backdrop blurs the page behind it
  - panels, cards and list entries get the glass rim
- **Later:** Mica behind the whole window in 6.2; the glass recording pill in 6.3.

### Contrast fix
WCAG AA, both themes. The new contrast check measures every visible text against the colours actually drawn. It found:
- **"Delete all" buttons in red,** dark theme: 4.35:1, already below AA before 6.1. Now `#f87171`.
- **Delete buttons in dialogs, white on red,** dark theme: 3.76:1, also older. Now a separate `--et-red-fill` (`#dc2626`).
- **Light-theme red and green** (first draft): 4.15:1 and 3.4:1. Now `#b91c1c` and `#166534`.

## 5. Known limitations

- **Glass is partial in 6.1:** dialogs, notifications and panel rims only. The window itself becomes glass with Mica in 6.2; the pill in 6.3.
- **The Transparency effects switch is re-read when EsyType's window comes to the front.** If it is changed while EsyType is already in front, the change shows the next time the window is activated.
- **The recording pill is always dark,** by design until its glass redesign in 6.3.
- **First-run setup has no theme step yet** (milestone 7.1); new users get Follow Windows.
- **The title bar colour comes from Windows** (dark or light caption). Its exact shade is Windows' own.
- **Windows 10:** themes work; the glass rules are the same; Mica (6.2) will not be available there.
- **Not yet checked by the owner** on a light Windows desktop. This PC runs dark mode, so Follow Windows was checked here only against dark; Light was checked by choosing it.
